# Audit output (polish-atlassian `--audit`)

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/style-guide.md` and `atlassian-formats.md` §5 "Making
it engaging without noise" first. Per page print:

- what works;
- problems ranked by reader impact (buried headline, hidden blocker, contradictions,
  grab-bag sections, plain-bullet criteria);
- Markdown/ADF options that fit;
- emoji recommendation (usually none, ⚠️ for a blocker);
- proposed outline in reader-priority order.
